# Pizza-Sales-Project-Using-SQL

Questions solved in this projects are--------
Ques- 1 ---- Retrieve the total number of orders placed(Calculate count of orderID).
Ques- 2 ----Calculate the total revenue generated from pizza sales.
Ques- 3 ----Identify the name of highest-priced pizza.
Ques- 4 ----Identify the most common pizza size ordered.
Ques- 5 ----List the top 5 most ordered pizza types along with their quantities.
Ques- 6 ----Join the necessary tables to find the total quantity of each pizza category ordered.
Ques- 7 ----Determine the distribution of orders by hour of the day.
Ques- 8 ----find the category-wise distribution of pizzas.
Ques- 9 ----Join relevant tables to find the category-wise distribution of pizzas.
Ques- 10 ----Group the orders by date and calculate the average number of pizzas ordered per day.
Ques- 11 ----Determine the top 3 most ordered pizza types based on revenue.
Ques- 12 ----Calculate the percentage contribution of each pizza type to total revenue.
